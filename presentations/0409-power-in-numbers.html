Power In Numbers

  • $N$ persons ($N \geq 3$).
  • Each person chooses to participate or not participate in a protest.
  • As a result of the protest, the ruling regime will be strengthened or weakened.
  • Let $P$ be the number of persons who participate in the protest. The probability with which the regime is weakened by the protest is $\frac{P}{1+P}$.

stories we can tell

Weakening the government is good, but a pain in the ass
Each person's utility is: \[ \begin{Bmatrix} 1 & \text{if the gov. is weakened} \\ -1 & \text{if the gov. is strengthened} \\ \end{Bmatrix} - \begin{Bmatrix} c & \text{if the person participates} \\ 0 & \text{if the person does not participate} \\ \end{Bmatrix} \]
I just want to be on the winning side
Each person's utility is: \[ \begin{Bmatrix} 1 & \text{if the gov. is weakened} \\ -1 & \text{if the gov. is strenghtened} \\ \end{Bmatrix} \times \begin{Bmatrix} w & \text{if the person participates} \\ -w & \text{if the person does not participate} \\ \end{Bmatrix} \]

Any one person's expected utility from each of her available actions:

If she participates:

\[ P(\text{gov weakened} \left| \text{she participates} \right.) U(\text{gov weakened and she participates}) + P(\text{gov strengthened} \left| \text{she participates} \right.)U(\text{gov strengthened and she participates}) \]

If she does not participate:

\[ P(\text{gov weakened} \left| \text{she does not participate} \right.) U(\text{gov weakened and she does not participate}) + P(\text{gov strengthened} \left| \text{she does not participate} \right.)U(\text{gov strengthened and she does not participate}) \]
Notice: None of these probabilities are defined unless the person has an expectation about how many of the other persons will participate!!!
